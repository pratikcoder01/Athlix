# Historical accuracy-guide path

The previous guide at this path promised unsupported model accuracy and implied
that training scripts established a validated classifier. Those claims were
removed. No independent accuracy benchmark or reviewed trained ATHLIX classifier
has been supplied.

The current analyzer provides experimental 2D pose estimates and heuristic
feedback. It does not recognize kata sequences, measure physical force, provide
medical conclusions, or guarantee a numerical skill/accuracy outcome.

Use the [current worker setup](cv-service/README.md) and the
[evaluation plan](docs/AI_EVALUATION.md). Training/data-collection scripts are
experimental utilities, not evidence of trained assets or product accuracy.
