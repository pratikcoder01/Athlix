# ATHLIX — monetization hypotheses

No working payment collection, recurring billing, paid entitlement, or settlement
ledger is demonstrated in this build. Pricing pages offer discussion paths,
not purchasable plans. Listed coach prices and tournament entry fees do not
establish gross merchandise value, collected revenue, or platform commission.

## Proposed order to test

1. **Organizer event fee:** discuss a fixed fee for a supervised local event after
   the core registration/bracket journey is reliable. Validate willingness to pay
   and support requirements before quoting a commercial commitment.
2. **Academy license:** consider recurring pricing only after a real repeat-use
   operational workflow exists. Member records, attendance, staff access, and
   school-specific permissions must work before being sold.
3. **Analysis usage:** consider a bounded, optional analysis allowance only after
   useful feedback, consent, retention, processing cost, and error rates are
   measured. Do not sell a validated coaching or medical outcome.

Athlete access should make registration easy; whether it remains free is a
commercial decision still to validate. No permanent-free or timed-trial promise
is currently offered.

## Unit economics to measure

Track actual event support hours, hosting/database/observability costs, failed
and successful analysis processing time, provider spend, acquisition effort,
onboarding time, repeat organizer usage, and refunds/support obligations if
payments are later enabled. Separate billed, collected, refunded, disputed, and
recognized amounts. Any forecast must state its assumptions and uncertainty.
There are no defensible CAC, LTV, ARR, margin, or market-size figures yet.

## Payment launch requirements

Before any paid offer: agree and review commercial/privacy terms, implement
server-created checkout, verified webhooks, idempotent event handling, an auditable
payment ledger, entitlements, cancellation/refund handling, failure recovery, and
sandbox acceptance tests. Confirm supported currency, taxes, provider terms, and
operator responsibilities with appropriate advisers. Keep the feature disabled
until reviewed and explicitly approved for release. No production payment or
billing settings were changed by this engineering work.
